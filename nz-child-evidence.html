04｜可直接发送的核验问题（英文）

To Immigration New Zealand: I am assessing a Guardian Visitor Visa for one parent accompanying a fee-paying school child. Before relying on local part-time earnings, please confirm: 1. For the proposed job [employer, location, duties, pay and roster], can the guardian apply for a variation to work within 09:30–14:30 Monday–Friday? 2. Is there a guardian-specific weekly limit, and which exact employer/job/location conditions would be recorded? 3. What process is required before changing employer or renewing the underlying guardian visa? 4. How should linked child and guardian applications show shared-family funds without double counting? 5. Can linked initial applications be lodged together, or must the child's student visa be granted first?
To a prospective employer: I do not currently have permission to start local work. I would need a conditional written offer and INZ approval before starting. I can only consider a roster within 09:30–14:30 Monday–Friday, with any further limits in my eVisa. Could you confirm guaranteed hours, breaks, location, transport requirements and whether you can wait for approval?

模板是研究核验工具，不是替家庭提供个案移民法律意见。[L07]
